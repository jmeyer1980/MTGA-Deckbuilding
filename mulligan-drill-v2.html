“Worked” means the hand plus its draws had 3–5 lands by turn four — enough to cast your curve without flooding.

Base rule: keep 2–5 lands, mulligan 0, 1, 6 or 7. Standard PG/PH: keep 2–5 only with a turn-1/2 board starter and at least 2 spells with mana value 3 or less (Ethereal Armor counts only with a creature in hand); Hares v2 also checks stranded payoffs, land bridges, and functional lands. It isn't always right — the ledger shows you exactly how often it beat your instinct, and how often it didn't.
